# Data Collection Framework

## Household Survey

Administered across Tee Garden, Chemuenaa, and Lante Maami (167 responses total).

| Variable | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Community / Town | Identify project location |
| Household demographics (size, gender, age of members) | Establish population reached |
| NHIS coverage | Track health insurance access |
| Malaria case history & trend vs. last year | Monitor malaria intervention outcomes |
| Mosquito net usage | Track prevention behavior |
| Toilet access, type, and construction source | Monitor sanitation infrastructure outcomes |
| Open defecation practice | Identify remaining sanitation risk |
| Waste bin access & disposal method | Monitor waste management outcomes |
| Drinking water source & treatment | Monitor WASH / cholera-prevention outcomes |
| Handwashing practices | Monitor hygiene behavior |
| Diarrheal case history & trend vs. last year | Monitor cholera/diarrheal intervention outcomes |
| Satisfaction & perceived improvement | Capture community perception of project impact |
| Remaining sanitation/health challenges | Identify open issues for follow-up |

## School Survey

Administered at 21 schools (Tee Garden). Covers enrollment, functional toilet facilities and their construction source, handwashing stations, soap availability, water source, malaria and diarrheal case counts, hygiene education, and perceived impact on student attendance.

## Data Flow

```
Form-based survey responses (Excel)
        │
        ▼
Cleaning & consolidation by community
        │
        ▼
Power BI dashboards (community-level filtering)
        │
        ▼
Summary findings & recommendations for the project team
```
